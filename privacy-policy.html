Acadmees Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Acadmees (the “App”) handles information when you use it. It covers information stored on your device and information processed through Firebase services used by the App.

1. Information We Handle

The information handled by Acadmees depends on the features you use. It may include:

  • Account information: information you provide when creating or managing an account, such as your name, email address, or other account details, if those fields are requested by the App.
  • App information: information you enter or create while using Acadmees, where the relevant feature stores that information.
  • Locally stored information: certain app data may be saved in a database on your device so that the App can retain information or provide its features.
  • Firebase-processed information: information required for account management and the App's backend features may be processed by Firebase. The exact information depends on which Firebase products and features are enabled in the App.

Do not enter sensitive personal information into the App unless a feature specifically requires it and explains why.

2. How We Use Information

Information handled by Acadmees may be used to:

  • create, authenticate, and manage user accounts;
  • provide the App's features and save app-related information;
  • keep information available between app sessions or devices where the relevant feature supports this;
  • maintain, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the App; and
  • respond to support requests.

3. Local Database and Firebase

Acadmees uses a local database on your device for certain app data. Data stored only locally generally remains on that device and may be removed if you clear the App's storage or uninstall the App. Device backups or operating-system behavior may affect this.

Acadmees also uses Firebase for account management and backend functionality. Firebase is provided by Google. Information processed through Firebase is subject to Google's applicable service terms and privacy information. Learn more at Firebase privacy and security and Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by Google/Firebase as a service provider to operate the App's account and backend features. We may also disclose information if required by applicable law or where reasonably necessary to protect the rights, safety, and security of users or the App.

This policy does not mean that every Firebase product is enabled. The App's actual data practices depend on the Firebase products and configuration included in the published version.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored information may remain on your device until you delete it, clear the App's storage, or uninstall the App. Information stored through Firebase may be retained for as long as needed to provide the App's services, maintain account records, resolve disputes, enforce applicable terms, or meet legal obligations.

To ask about access to or deletion of information associated with your account, contact us using the email address below. We may need to verify your request before acting on it. Some information may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate security purposes.

6. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by the App. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy

Parents or guardians who have questions or concerns about information a child may have provided through Acadmees can contact us using the details below. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child in circumstances where parental consent is legally required, we will take appropriate steps to address it.

8. Your Choices

You can choose what information you provide to the App, subject to the information required for particular features. You can remove locally stored data through your device settings or by uninstalling the App. You may also contact us with questions or requests concerning information associated with your account.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's features, data practices, or legal requirements change. Any updated version will be published at this same URL, with a revised effective date.
